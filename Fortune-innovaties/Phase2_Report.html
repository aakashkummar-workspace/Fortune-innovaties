2. Bug Fixes & Corrections

The following bugs were identified and resolved during the Phase 2 release cycle. Each is documented with symptom, root cause, and the fix that was deployed.

Fix 1 — Telecaller dashboard showed entire branch leads
Commit 044e118 · Severity: High · Affected: Telecaller dashboards

Symptom: Both Telecaller 1 and Telecaller 2 (both Tiruppur) were each seeing identical 1,000-lead pipelines and identical "Total Assigned Leads" modal contents — defeating the round-robin distribution from FR-15.

Root cause: The getLeads endpoint was using where.OR = [{ assignedToId }, { branchId }] for telecaller roles, which leaked every branch lead to every telecaller in the branch.

Fix: Strict filter — where.assignedToId = req.user.id for the TELECALLER role. Each telecaller now sees only their own ~600 leads.

Fix 2 — Trainer saw another trainer's row on co-taught batches
Commit 5dd775c · Severity: Medium · Affected: Trainer timetable view

Symptom: When trainer A was primary on a batch with trainer B as secondary, both rows rendered in trainer A's timetable. Trainers also couldn't see batches where they were the secondary.

Fix: where filter now uses OR [trainerId, secondaryTrainerId]; row builder only adds a row when the current user is the relevant trainer on that batch.

Fix 3 — "LOG LATE" button missing on missed slots
Commit bb9f3c6 · Severity: Medium · Affected: Trainer logging UX

Symptom: Once a slot was auto-marked MISSED, trainers had no way to retroactively log what they actually taught.

Fix: Red "LOG LATE" button now appears on missed slots. Submit flips status back to LOGGED via existing upsert.

Fix 4 — Missed-hour alert fired too early
Commit b497e57 · Severity: High · Affected: All reviewer roles

Symptom: Red "missed teaching hour" alert fired the moment each 2-hour slot ended, even though trainers expected all day to log.

Fix: Constant END_OF_SHIFT_MIN = 1200 (8 PM IST). Scheduler early-returns before that; after 8 PM, unlogged scheduled slots are marked MISSED once and CEO/ADMIN/CP notified.

Fix 5 — CEO/CP could not see free-hour logs
Commit 0533df6 · Severity: Medium

Symptom: Green "Free hour" tiles trainers logged were invisible to reviewers.

Fix: Backend now fetches free-hour logs for all trainers in scope and attaches them per-trainer as freeHourBySlot. CEO/CP see all (read-only); trainer remains the only one who can edit their own.

Fix 6 — Log modal had heavy dark backdrop
Commit 6ce4811 · Severity: Low (UX polish)

Fix: Dark overlay replaced with fully transparent backdrop. Modal still floats with a soft shadow and ring so it reads as elevated.

Fix 7 — Company & Student dropdowns clipped long lists
Commit f7f7951 · Severity: Low

Fix: SelectContent now has max-h-72 with internal scrolling.

3. Feature — FR-08 Student UPI Self-Pay

STUDENT (pay) CEO (verify) Page: Student Dashboard → Fees tab

Students can now pay their remaining fees directly from their dashboard by scanning the CEO's UPI QR code (auto-generated from the institute's UPI ID) and submitting the resulting Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) number for manual verification. The CEO approves or rejects each submission, and the student sees status updates in real time.

Manual verification — NOT Razorpay

Per the requirement, this is intentionally a manual UPI flow (CEO scans bank statement, matches the UTR, then approves in the CRM). No payment gateway fees, no PCI scope, no API integration burden. Just a managed verification workflow.

3.1 Student-facing flow

Figure 3.1 — Student Dashboard Fees tab. Left: pay card with auto-generated UPI QR + 12-digit UTR input. Right: history of submitted payments with status banners.

3.2 UTR validation rules

LayerValidationBehavior on failure
Client (input)Auto-strips non-digit characters; caps at 12 charsSubmit button disabled, inline error "Enter all 12 digits"
Server (regex)/^\d{12}$/HTTP 400 "Invalid UTR format"
Server (uniqueness)UTR cannot match any existing APPROVED paymentHTTP 409 "This UTR is already approved for another payment"

3.3 Step-by-step workflow

  1. Student opens dashboard → Fees tab. Card "Pay Remaining Fees" shows the current balance from admission.feeBalance.
  2. UPI QR is auto-generated using the institute UPI ID (from TermsSettings) via api.qrserver.com. Amount is pre-filled, so the student's UPI app shows the exact figure on scan.
  3. Student scans QR with any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and completes payment from their phone.
  4. Their bank sends a confirmation SMS with the UTR (a 12-digit number, e.g. 324871558019).
  5. Student types the UTR into the input on the dashboard. Live validation guides them to exactly 12 digits.
  6. On Submit, the backend creates a FeePaymentRequest with status PENDING and attaches the UTR.
  7. The card flips to "Waiting for approval" status. A new entry appears in "My Submitted Payments" with the orange Waiting badge.
  8. The page auto-refreshes every 30 seconds and on window focus — so the student sees CEO action without manual reload.
  9. CEO opens the Fees → Approvals page, sees the request with the UTR shown, matches it against the bank statement, and clicks Approve or Reject.
  10. Approval → the badge flips to green "Approved", and admission.feeBalance is decremented. Rejection → red badge with the CEO's reason.
Real conversation flow

Anandhi V owes ₹ 12,500. She opens her phone, scans the QR on her student dashboard, transfers ₹ 12,500 to fortune@upi via Google Pay, and receives an SMS: "Sent ₹12,500 to FORTUNE INNOVATIVES. UTR: 324871558019." She types 324871558019 into the input field, submits. The CEO receives a notification, opens her bank statement on a laptop, confirms the UTR matches, clicks Approve. Anandhi's screen shows "Approved" within 30 seconds. Her balance drops to zero.

7. Feature — FR-13 Attendance Audit Log

CEO ADMIN CHANNEL PARTNER Page: /attendance/audit Commit: b8e2175

Every attendance write across students, trainers, and staff is now permanently recorded with a full audit trail. The system captures who pressed the button, when, what was set, what changed (before → after), the method (manual, QR, GPS, self check-in, or leave sync), the source endpoint, IP address, and user agent of the requester.

7.1 Use cases

Dispute resolution

A student claims they were present on 25 June but were marked absent. The CEO filters by Kind=Student, Date=25 Jun, searches the student's name. The result shows exactly who marked the student absent, from which device, via which method, at what time. Informed correction follows.

Detect back-dating

A trainer tries to retroactively mark Monday's attendance at Friday 11 PM. The audit log captures the action timestamp (Friday 11 PM) and the attendance date (Monday) — the mismatch is permanently visible.

9. Feature — FR-15 Equal Lead Distribution

TELECALLER (receives leads) Backend: createPublicLead

Public-form leads (submitted by prospective students via the website) now auto-distribute to the available telecallers using a round-robin least-loaded strategy. The previous behavior dumped all public leads onto the CEO's queue, which then required manual reassignment.

9.1 Distribution algorithm

  1. When a public lead arrives via POST /api/leads/public, the backend runs a groupBy query counting active leads per telecaller.
  2. The telecaller with the fewest currently-assigned active leads wins (lowest count). In case of a tie, the first one returned by the query is picked.
  3. The new lead's assignedToId is set to that telecaller's user ID.
  4. The telecaller receives a notification: "New lead assigned: [Name]" with a click-through to the lead detail.
Historical redistribution

A one-off migration was run during the rollout to redistribute 188 existing leads that had been incorrectly assigned to the CEO. The script re-allocated them by round-robin between Telecaller 1 and Telecaller 2, resulting in TC1=605 and TC2=604 active leads (a difference of just one, which is the theoretical optimum for an odd total of 1,209).

9.2 Why this matters

BeforeAfter
All ~1,000 public leads landed in CEO's queue. CEO manually reassigned each one or telecallers had to fish for unclaimed leads. Public leads instantly land in whichever telecaller's queue is currently smallest. CEO's queue stays at zero. Telecallers see new leads as soon as they're submitted.
No accountability — if a lead sat unworked, the CEO got the watchlist warning even though no telecaller had been assigned. Accountability is automatic — every public lead has a real owner from the moment it's created, and the Accountability Watchlist correctly flags the assigned telecaller if it goes stale.

10. Feature — FR-16 Placement Readiness 2-Step Approval

TRAINER (submit) CEO (approve) ADMIN (approve) CP (own branch) Pages: /placement-readiness · /placements

Placement submissions from trainers no longer land directly in the placement queue. They sit in a "Pending Approvals" tab where CEO/ADMIN/CP review, approve, or reject with a written reason. The branch placement counter is only incremented after approval. Five sub-features support the core flow.

10.2 CEO Pending Approvals queue with readiness pills

Figure 10.2 — CEO Pending Approvals with readiness pills inline. Contrast Anandhi (mostly green, easy approve) vs kavya (red attendance + outstanding fee — needs scrutiny).

10.3 Approve / Reject flow

Approve
  1. CEO clicks the green Approve button.
  2. Backend: status = ELIGIBLE, approvedById + approvedAt set, branch totalPlacements atomically incremented.
  3. Trainer receives a notification "Placement readiness approved" with a link to /placements.
  4. Row disappears from Pending; appears in the regular All Placements view.
Reject
  1. CEO clicks red Reject — modal opens with required reason textarea (min 3 chars).
  2. Backend: status = REJECTED, rejectedAt + rejectionReason set (capped at 500 chars). Counter NOT incremented.
  3. Trainer receives a notification with the rejection reason snippet.
  4. Trainer's "My Submissions" table shows the row as REJECTED with the reason visible on hover.
Idempotency & state guards

Both endpoints verify status === PENDING_READINESS before flipping. An already-approved or already-rejected record cannot be re-processed (returns HTTP 400). Branch counter is guaranteed to increment exactly once.

12. Feature — FR-17B Slot Logging & Missed-Hour Alerts

TRAINER (log) CEO (review) ADMIN (review) CP (review)

Trainers log what they taught in each 2-hour slot through the day. Empty slots get a "free hour" log option for non-batch work. At end of shift (8 PM IST), any unlogged scheduled slot triggers a red alert to CEO, ADMIN, and branch CP.

12.1 The four slot states

Figure 12.1 — Four slot states. LOGGED (green ✓), PENDING (blue + LOG button), MISSED (red + LOG LATE), FUTURE (neutral).

12.2 End-of-shift detection rule

The 8 PM IST rule

Past slots stay in PENDING state throughout the working day. The scheduler runs every 5 minutes but does NO work before 8 PM IST. This gives trainers the entire day to catch up. Only after 8 PM does the scheduler scan for unlogged scheduled slots and mark them MISSED, fire notifications, and surface red alerts.

12.3 Free-hour logging

Empty cells in the trainer's row show a dashed "+ Log free hour" button. Trainers click it to record administrative, preparation, or other non-batch work. The cell flips to a green "Free hour" tile. CEO, ADMIN, and CP see these tiles read-only for all trainers in scope.

A typical Tuesday for Keerthi

FI_GDC_01 (8:30-10:30): logged "Pattern Hierarchy ch.3". FI_PD_12 online (10:30-12:30): logged. 12-2 PM free: logged "Reviewed portfolio submissions". FI_TGD_10 (2-4 PM): logged. 4-6 PM free: logged "Built slide deck for tomorrow". 6-8 PM: empty, no work. At end of day, the CEO can see exactly how Keerthi used every hour of the day.

12.4 Three coordinated red surfaces for reviewers

  • CEO Dashboard tile — "Missed Teaching Hours Today" with a red counter, clickable to /timetable
  • Timetable page banner — red callout at the top with expandable per-trainer drill-down
  • Red chips in the grid — each missed slot is itself visually marked

12.5 Daily history view

Figure 12.2 — Date navigator strip. All four roles can browse past days; future days disabled at the picker.

All four roles (CEO, ADMIN, CP, Trainer) can scroll back to past days to see historic logs and missed entries. Past days are strictly read-only — log buttons hidden, free-hour tiles non-clickable. Auto-refresh is disabled for past days.

13.1 Low-rating alert

  1. Student rates trainer 1 or 2 stars from their dashboard.
